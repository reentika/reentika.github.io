May

An Endless Month Filled with Learning & Celebrations


The end of April, I began a goal I had been wanting to complete since I was in Grade 10, and that was a website! I embarked on the journey to create a website from scratch, and finally made it! Along the way, I decided to make it even more personal by making it Netflix-themed. One of my goals is to work at Netflix one day. This came from my love for cinema, and TV shows when I started recognizing how well the algorithm worked for me in recommending me TV shows/movies.


This was probably my busiest month of the year. From painting the walls of my new house, to graduating from the cadet program, and volunteering at a conference. It was VERY hectic.


From One Wall to the Whole House…

What started as a simple mission between my sister and me — just repainting our bedroom walls — quickly turned into a full-house makeover. After moving into a new home, we noticed scuff marks and patches on the walls and thought, why not freshen things up a bit? I had some free time, and after binging way too many ASMR painting videos, I figured this was the perfect moment to channel that energy into something productive.


After we painted our first wall white, we got way too confident. Next thing you know, we were experimenting with colors, textures, and even wallpapers. Our DIY painting project spiraled into a two-week adventure. And though it took a surprising amount of effort, sweat, and trial-and-error (seriously — wallpaper is not as easy as it looks), we wrapped it up with a real sense of accomplishment.


Before & After of my room - still not complete BTW!


You might be wondering why take on a project like this in my only three weeks off before summer school? The truth is, I thrive when I’m busy. There’s so much to learn, create, and experience. And I believe if you can take initiative on the small things, you build the mindset and to take on bigger ones too.


So, if you’ve been thinking about repainting your room; just go for it. Use the resources availble, spend a little, and make something your own. It’s worth it. 🙂



Sylvie Brought Me Water

In the middle of all the house chaos, I officially aged out of the cadet program! I joined back in October 2019 as a shy 13-year-old, unsure of what I was getting into, fueled by my father’s passion from the Indian Navy. And now, after years of showing up, learning, and growing, I can say with confidence: I got so much out of it.


Getting my mug!


The cadet program truly has something for everyone. It took me a while to find what I loved most and to be honest, I will ALWAYS wish I had explored even more. But with the opportunities I did take, I’m incredibly proud. By the end, I earned the rank of CPO2 and contributed to my corps in meaningful ways, especially through social media. Someday, I’d love to go back and womp-womp with my friends and beg sylvie for water 😉


I met amazing friends, gained real-life skills, and created memories I’ll always hold close. If you're in the program (or thinking of joining), I hope you make the most of it. Show up. Stay curious. It’s worth it.


And of course, a huge shoutout to my second favorite dessert spot, Chocolate Favoris, for opening just in time on May 3rd and being the perfect place to celebrate my age-out. Love you forever!

June

Reformative June


The beginning of June was marked with a celebration of the age-outs of my friends and the mid-way mark of the year that began my goal in self-growth. I cannot believe it took me nearly half a year to recognize that my lifestyle needed to have some changes. Enjoy reading how I began changing my lifestyle!


Days in my Life

An Instagram account that started as a joke between my sister and I pretending to be top-tier content creators transformed into an opportunity for me to monitor myself and the changes I can make in my lifestyle. I started filming clips of almost every task I was completing in a day, whether it be brushing my teeth, meal-prepping, or attending my lecture. I then took these clips and added narration to finally create a product of “Day in my Life.” My entire audience was my family as I don’t feel comfortable sharing that with strangers on the internet. But you may be wondering why? Well, towards the end of May, I noticed I was getting very lethargic and I genuinely could not keep track of stuff I did and didn’t do. Most importantly, the checklists I was creating myself before sleeping the night before or in the morning were not getting ticked. This led me to wonder what I am actually doing in my day.

And making those videos, I noticed I was taking wayy too many naps in a day. But most importantly I wasn’t even staying on tasks. I wasn’t completing any school work because I was doing some other “sidequest” that wasn’t as important as my piled up WebWork assignments.

So, it was time to fix that. By noticing what activities I did, I started making small changes which led to my days getting more and more productive, and soon my clips were getting shorter, meaning I didn’t have time to film the stuff I was doing because it was a) too boring (like studying lol) or b) genuinely didn’t get time to bring out my phone since I was so focused.

Slowly, I am becoming more and more mindful of my choices. So yay me! But the most important thing I’ve learned from this is that routine is extremely important. Getting up on time, sleeping on time, attending lectures, exercising, meal prepping, and spending time with family and friends, these are the things that can make a routine, and believe it or not it is what is important for your well-being.



Design Team Dubs

Amidst all the first year engineering stress at UBC, I joined UBC Sailbot. Driven with a lot of passion and hope, I started off with doing any task available whenever I could. But somewhere, that first year stress caught up to me. Even though I participated in any which way through researching parts or concepts, I wasn’t feeling satisfied with my work, I wanted to do more. And this summer was when I thought it to be the best time for it. So, I wrapped up a project my lead gave to me and I was extremely proud of the outcome. This project was so good in that it wrapped so many different learnings with it.

The project was to create a wire fatigue tester, by the sound of it, it seemed a bit pointless, but still I motivated myself to do it. With that, I learned a lot about the importance of testing and iteration of designs:

  1. C++ Coding - coding an arduino to have a push-pull mechanism
  2. 3D Printing - had to create my own attachment for the wire to go through
  3. Python - using this to make it so that the data is put into a CSV File for easier use and understanding.
  4. Problem Solving - in the future as an engineer, I knew that multiple systems, need to test things and what not

What started as a simple project, became a multi-faceted one with many layers of frustration, but most importantly learning. I look forward to continuing working in such ways.
